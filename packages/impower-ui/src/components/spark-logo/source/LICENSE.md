# Spark Engine logo, wordmark and splash

Sparky, the Spark Engine wordmark and the "Made with Spark" splash are the maintainer's own work, copied unchanged from the `logo/` folder of the private Spark Engine design repository, and may be published. The design repository's restriction on redistributing art covers only its demo project's third-party art, not these files.

- `spark_engine_logo_cutout.svg`: Sparky with his eyes cut out.
- `spark_engine_wordmark.svg`, `spark_engine_wordmark_on_dark.svg`: the two-line "spark engine" wordmark for light and dark grounds.
- `made_with_spark_splash.svg`: the animated "Made with Spark" splash.

`impower-dev/scripts/generate-icons.mjs` renders the app icons, favicon and launch image from these files in Charcoal's colours.
