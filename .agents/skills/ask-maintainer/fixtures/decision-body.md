## The short version

When the preview works out how the story reaches the line you're on, it has to pick an option at every choice you haven't pinned. The script's left edge shows a small mark on each option it picked. This asks what the mark should be, or whether there should be one at all.

## What you're looking at

<img src="./phone.png" width="300" alt="Phone screenshot of the script with a bulleted-list icon in the left edge">

- **The route.** The thin coloured strip down the left edge is the path the preview took.
- **The mark.** The option the preview took gets a mark.

## The options

### A · Keep the bulleted-list icon

<img src="./a.png" width="420" alt="A filled pin on the first option and a bulleted-list icon on the second">

- **Pros:** already built, and a different shape from a pin.
- **Cons:** reads as "a menu", not "the preview chose this".
- **To undo later:** nothing to undo.
